# APRISM Digital Brand

## Positioning

**Luxury Asset Stewardship**  
**Managing What Matters.**

APRISM is calm, discreet, exacting, and accountable. It supports the full operational history of valuable properties and specialty assets rather than presenting itself as a repair or handyman service.

## Core differentiator

- One relationship.
- One property record.
- One maintenance strategy.
- One accountable steward.

## Voice

Write with quiet confidence. Prefer specific operational language: condition, continuity, record, stewardship, observation, coordination, and follow-through.

Avoid bargain language, handyman clichés, inflated superlatives, exaggerated luxury language, excessive punctuation, and urgency not supported by the condition.

## Color

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#0B0D0D` | Primary public background |
| Carbon | `#111414` | Elevated dark surfaces |
| Limestone | `#EFEDE6` | Editorial light surfaces |
| Paper | `#F7F5EE` | Cards and portal panels |
| Warm gold | `#C7A76B` | Primary action and restrained brand accent |
| Deep ink | `#171A19` | Text and dark action surfaces |

Gold should remain an accent, never a decorative wash across every element.

## Typography

- Cormorant Garamond for headlines and property names
- Manrope for navigation, labels, forms, and body copy
- Uppercase labels use generous tracking and small sizes
- Headlines favor short, measured lines and substantial whitespace

Fonts are loaded through `next/font` for self-hosted production assets and stable rendering.

## Layout and interaction

- Mobile-first spacing with touch targets of at least 44px
- Large editorial headlines balanced by concise supporting copy
- Thin rules and low-contrast panels instead of heavy shadows or rounded consumer-app cards
- Motion is subtle and disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Portal status colors always include a text label; color is never the only signal

## Imagery

Use authentic Park City mountain residences, architectural details, building systems, and specialty assets. Favor quiet dawn/dusk light, material texture, winter readiness, and documentary detail. Avoid generic contractors, tools-in-hand stock photos, or theatrical luxury staging.

The MVP uses restrained CSS atmospheric landscapes so the experience is complete without unlicensed placeholder photography. Final photography can replace these fields without changing the design system.
